## First researcher prompt

> Read this brief and the Legal-AI workflow. Start with exact lookup and keyword search in the local index. Record the queries, filters, and snapshot version. Use public sources or provider tools to fill identified court and date gaps. Gather supporting and adverse authorities. Return candidate citations with source links and pinpoints, along with gaps and later treatment to verify.

## Independent reviewer prompt

> Start with the brief only. Search independently for governing, adverse, and later authority. List the tools and sources you actually accessed. For each result, give the citation, court, date, pinpoint, what the court decided, and what limits the result. Label leads you could not verify. Submit this first pass before receiving the other researcher’s results.

## Drafting prompt

> Draft the requested deliverable from the reconciled evidence packet. Cite verified primary authority with a pinpoint for each material legal proposition. Preserve applicable dates, jurisdiction limits, exceptions, procedural posture, and adverse authorities. Attribute disputed facts and show what remains open. Flag claims that the packet cannot support.
